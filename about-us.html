UGI GROUP OF INSTITUTIONS & EDUCATION

North India’s Fastest Growing Institution-Universal Group of Institutions, Lalru (Mohali)

Universal Group of institutions was established in 2009 . Its foundation was laid by eminent educationists and renowned scholars and with the sole aim to bestow top quality education. It provides numerous courses in various disciplines such as Law, Management, Nursing, Humanities, Education, Pharmacy, Engineering and Polytechnic. UGI has been the hub of quality education ever since it was set up. UGI is the most suitable and conducive place to study as it is located at the most ideal place in the vicinity of the city of Chandigarh . The aura of the UGI campus is the most conducive for educational learning. Placing thrust on hands-on learning, the infrastructure provided here is ultra modern along with most sophisticated classrooms and laboratories for practical tasks. The UGI is renowned for its expert staff and faculty, the use of digital and ultra modern technology. Additionally, the emphasis is always on challenge-based case studies and research and development in order to make education relevant to ever-changing career dynamics. Alongside the placement record of UGI is also an exceptional one.

For over a quarter-century milestone, UGI has been a leader in providing world-class education, preparing students to excel in competitive industries. As we approach moving forward into 2026, our institution continues to focus on students all over development, combining top-tier academics with real-world experience to ensure that students are ready to pursue the careers of their dreams.

Apart from the quality education, our education goes beyond theoretical learning. By providing practical knowledge and professional growth, students at UGI have emerged as well-rounded professionals. At present, UGI has attained a remarkable success in placements.
